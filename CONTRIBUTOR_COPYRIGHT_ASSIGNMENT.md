## Recitals

A. The Contributor created the Project and all Contributions thereto in the
course of and within the scope of their employment by the Company.

B. Under applicable law, including Article 17 of the Swiss Federal Act on
Copyright and Related Rights (*Urheberrechtsgesetz*, URG / *Loi fédérale sur
le droit d'auteur et les droits voisins*, LDA), copyright in computer programs
created by an employee in the course of their professional duties and following
the employer's instructions vests automatically and exclusively in the employer.
The Company therefore owns all Intellectual Property Rights in and to the
Contributions by operation of law, without the need for any further act of
assignment.

C. The parties wish to record and confirm that ownership and, to the extent
that any rights have not already vested in the Company by operation of law, to
assign them to the Company by way of this Agreement so that the Company's title
is placed beyond doubt.

## Agreement

In consideration of the mutual promises contained herein and other good and
valuable consideration, the receipt and adequacy of which are acknowledged,
the parties agree as follows.

---

### 1. Definitions

1.1 **"Contributions"** means all works of authorship — including source code,
object code, documentation, configuration files, build scripts, test files,
and any other materials — that the Contributor authored or co-authored and
committed or otherwise contributed to the Project repository at any time before
or after the date of this Agreement, whether under the Contributor's
`thomas.gruet@saitis.net` identity, the `thomas.gruet@nimag.net` identity, or
any other identity.

1.2 **"Intellectual Property Rights"** means all present and future rights of
copyright and neighbouring rights, moral rights (to the extent waivable),
database rights, and any other intellectual-property rights recognised in any
jurisdiction, together with all renewals, extensions, and reversions thereof.

---

### 2. Confirmation of Ownership and Assignment

2.1 The parties confirm and acknowledge that all Intellectual Property Rights
in and to the Contributions vest in the Company by operation of law (in
particular Article 17 URG/LDA) as a result of the Contributions having been
created by the Contributor within the scope of their employment by the Company.

2.2 To the extent — if any — that any Intellectual Property Rights in the
Contributions have not vested in the Company by operation of law, the
Contributor hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company, with full title
guarantee and free from all encumbrances, all such rights throughout the world
for the full term of those rights (including any extensions or renewals).

2.3 The assignment in clause 2.2 includes, without limitation, the exclusive
right to:

- (a) reproduce, copy, and distribute the Contributions in any medium and by
  any means;
- (b) prepare and distribute derivative works based on the Contributions;
- (c) publicly perform and display the Contributions;
- (d) **license and relicense the Contributions under any licence terms**,
  including proprietary, commercial, open-source, dual, or any other
  licensing model, whether or not more or less restrictive than any licence
  previously applied to the Project;
- (e) sublicense any of the foregoing rights to third parties; and
- (f) assign or transfer any of the foregoing rights to third parties.

2.4 This Agreement applies retrospectively to all Contributions made prior to
its date as well as to all future Contributions made after it.

### 3. Licence-Back to Contributor

3.1 The Company hereby grants to the Contributor a perpetual, worldwide,
irrevocable, royalty-free, non-exclusive licence to use, reproduce, modify,
and distribute the Contributions for personal, academic, or open-source
purposes under the terms of the GNU General Public License version 3 (or any

open-source licence approved by the Open Source Initiative.

3.2 The licence-back in clause 3.1 does not include the right to sublicense
the Contributions under proprietary or commercial terms; that right remains

3.3 The licence-back in clause 3.1 takes effect only upon termination of the
Contributor's employment by the Company. During active employment, the
Contributor's use of the Contributions is governed by their employment
contract and applicable Company policies.

### 4. Moral Rights

4.1 To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, the Contributor waives
all moral rights (including the right of attribution and the right of
integrity) in the Contributions in favour of the Company and its licensees and
assigns.

4.2 Where a waiver of moral rights is not permitted by law, the Contributor
agrees not to assert those rights against the Company, its licensees, or
assigns in connection with the Company's exercise of the rights described in
this Agreement.

### 5. Representations and Warranties

The Contributor represents and warrants that:

5.1 all Contributions were created by the Contributor within the scope of
their employment by the Company, or — where any Contribution includes material
authored by a third party — the Contributor has obtained all rights necessary
to enable the Company to exercise the rights described in this Agreement;

5.2 the Contributions do not, to the best of the Contributor's knowledge,
infringe any third party's Intellectual Property Rights;

5.3 the Contributions are not subject to any lien, charge, or encumbrance that
would restrict the Company's ownership or the assignment in clause 2.2; and

5.4 the Contributor has full legal capacity and authority to enter into this
Agreement.

### 6. Further Assurances

The Contributor shall, at the Company's reasonable request and expense, execute
and deliver all further documents and do all further acts reasonably necessary

and to register or record the Company's title in any relevant jurisdiction.

between the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior
